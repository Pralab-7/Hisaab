Common questions

Does Hisaab need the internet? No. Everything works offline.

Where are my history and saved conversions? Tap the history button at the top of the calculator. It has two tabs: Calculations and Saved.

How are land units converted? From exact relationships: 1 Ropani = 16 Aana = 5,476 sq ft, 1 Aana = 4 Paisa = 342.25 sq ft, 1 Paisa = 4 Daam, 1 Bigha = 20 Kattha = 72,900 sq ft, 1 Kattha = 20 Dhur = 3,645 sq ft, and 1 ft = 0.3048 m. Settings → Conversion sources lists where each factor comes from. Results are for everyday use. For legal or registration purposes, follow the Land Revenue (Malpot) office and your official documents.

Is the VAT rate always current? The VAT tool lets you type any rate. The 13% shortcut is Nepal's standard rate. Rates can change, so check the rate that applies to your bill.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Your data, or uninstall the app. See the privacy policy.
