Hisaab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · App: Hisaab (हिसाब) by Pralab Dahal · Contact: hisabnepalapp@gmail.com

In short

Hisaab does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It works fully offline and has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your calculation history (the latest 200), conversions you save (including any land rate, advance and balance you saved with them), your pinned and recent tools, and your preferences (language, digits, number grouping, decimal places, theme, haptics). These are kept only in the app's private storage on your device.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Your data lets you clear history, saved conversions and pinned tools, or reset preferences. Uninstalling the app removes everything. If your phone's backup (Android backup or iCloud) is on, your phone may include app data in its own backup; that is controlled by your phone's settings.

What Hisaab never does

No sign-in. No internet requests: the Android app does not request the Internet permission. No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access. No advertising identifiers. No third-party analytics or crash reporting.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the text you chose goes to your phone's share sheet and you decide where it goes. Hisaab does not see or keep where you send it.

Children

Hisaab is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Third-party components

The app is built with Flutter and the open-source packages flutter_riverpod, decimal, rational, intl, shared_preferences (on-device storage) and share_plus (the system share sheet). None of them collects or sends data in the way Hisaab uses them.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the release notes.

तपाईंको फोनमै रहने कुरा

डाटा मेटाउने

एपको सेटिङ → तपाईंको डाटाबाट इतिहास, सुरक्षित रूपान्तरण र पिन गरिएका उपकरण मेटाउन वा सेटिङ रिसेट गर्न सकिन्छ। एप हटाउँदा सबै मेटिन्छ। फोनको ब्याकअप (Android backup वा iCloud) खुला छ भने फोनले आफ्नै ब्याकअपमा एपको डाटा राख्न सक्छ।

हिसाबले कहिल्यै नगर्ने कुरा

साइन इन छैन। इन्टरनेट अनुरोध छैन। स्थान, सम्पर्क, क्यामेरा, माइक्रोफोन वा फोटो पहुँच छैन। विज्ञापन आईडी छैन। तेस्रो पक्षको एनालिटिक्स वा क्र्यास रिपोर्टिङ छैन।
